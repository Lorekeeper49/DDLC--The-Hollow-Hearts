# I am Stupid

I call myself that
all the time.
I am not a that,
it is my prime.

I am not a sage.
I don't know a page.
I'm not in a cage.
Someone got a guage?

I need some help
for a phase.
I've only dealt
with a daze.

My mind goes dead.
I've been sped.
I'm just bread.
I need a head.

I can do stories.
I can't do poems.
