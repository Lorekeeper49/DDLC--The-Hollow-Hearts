# Before the Past

Before the past,
what is there?
After the future,
what exists?

Nothing can exist
before the past.
Nothing can exist
after the future.

So what is there?

I know the prefix
before the prefix.
I know the suffix
after the suffix.

Before life
we were none.
Before the past
we were war

After life
we are none.
After the future
we are war.

It's all the same
in an endless cycle.
